# Anota

**The PDF reader where your notes live beside the page.**

Anota is a web PDF reader for people who read to think: researchers, students and writers.
Open a PDF, highlight what matters, and your notes sit right next to the passage they came from.

- **Notes beside the page.** Tag each one as an Insight, an Inquiry, an Action or General.
- **AI explanations.** Ask for a plain explanation of a hard passage without leaving the page.
- **Your notes are yours.** Export everything to Markdown on every plan, including Free.
- **Local-first, backed up to your account.** Pick up where you left off on any browser.

Free to start: 5 PDFs and 3 AI explanations a day. An Android app is coming in December 2026.
